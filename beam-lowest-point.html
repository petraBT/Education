Where does the beam sag most?

A 10 ft beam rests on two supports. Move the person along it and watch the lowest point of the beam.

The bending moment (bottom) always peaks right under the person. The sag peaks where the beam stops going down and starts coming back up, where its slope is zero. That point sits on the longer, floppier side, between the person and the middle. The two only coincide when the person stands in the center.

Bending is drawn exaggerated so the shape is easy to see.
